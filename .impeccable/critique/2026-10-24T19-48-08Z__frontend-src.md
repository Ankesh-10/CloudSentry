## Design specificity
Mostly generic: sidebar + bento cards + KPI sparklines + pastel badges + drawers, with real safety parts (automation pill, Live banner, persistent Emergency stop, ConfirmDialog consequence block, two-person notice) layered on top rather than shaping the layout. The Overview hero is a spend chart, not agent state. The detector is clean (CLI 0 findings); the overlay flagged one true positive: 10px "Demo" badge, Layout.tsx:246.

## Priority issues
1. [P0] Danger copy is wrong at the moments that need reassurance. ConfirmDialog hard-codes "Live change" and "This changes real cloud resources" (Dialog.tsx:73,87) and applies them to emergency stop, every rollback including dry-run records, and approvals while automation is off. Fix: work out the effective consequence; give stop a calm "safety" tone; hide rollback on dry-run; drop autoFocus on danger buttons. -> clarify + harden
2. [P1] Approvals don't explain before they ask. No reason, anomaly or policy on approve rows or dialogs; `Action.anomaly_id` is never used. Fix: a "Why" line plus a link to the anomaly, a readable expected change, and the checks that will run. -> clarify
3. [P1] The Overview hierarchy is upside down. Spend, figures, savings and timeline come before "Needs your attention" (Dashboard.tsx:342-346); it sits 3.3 screens down on a phone. Fix: agent-state strip, then attention list, then "since your last visit", then charts. -> layout
4. [P1] Phone width loses safety state and controls. Pills hidden below md; Stop shrinks to an icon (40x32); Approve sits off-screen in an 857px-wide table; 15/27 targets under 44px on /, 30/59 on /actions. Fix: compact state chip, keep "Stop", stacked action cards. -> adapt
5. [P1] Solo-operator dead end. The two-person rule blocks a lone user from re-arming after a stop or turning dry-run off, with no acknowledgement. Pending state is component-only (System.tsx:31,53) and lost on reload. -> shape
